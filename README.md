# HYPERMEDIA PROJECT, PT. 1: Documentation

## Project analysis:


> 1. User profile


- Andrés, 20 years old - Computer Engineering student

- Andrés is a third-year Computer Engineering student who wants to gain professional experience while completing his degree. He is looking for entry-level office jobs and internships related to computer science or other technology fields. His main goals are, for instance, to find opportunities and his first professional job, present his technical skills and academic background, and make a positive first impression on recruiters by showing that he is organized and responsible in his work.

- In order to achieve all that, he needs a professional and well-structured CV that is clear and easy to read. For him, it is essential for the website to show the programming languages, software tools, and technical knowledge that he has learnt. As well as that, it is important to provide the contact information and his professional background (education, work experience, certifications…).

- Unfortunately, as a student with limited professional experience, he needs to highlight his academic achievements and technical abilities. Therefore, he also needs to present his profile professionally so recruiters can quickly identify his strengths. For this reason, the CV website provides a structured overview of the user profile, combining a clear and attractive visual design with organized sections. Overall, this website will be very useful for him to communicate his academic results and achievements, show his technical knowledge and first work experiences, and make his profile accessible to recruiters.


> 2. Information architecture


- First of all, as the assignment instructions said, I designed the website as a single-page static CV.

- I have organized the information into three main areas: the profile description header, the user identity and languages section in the centre, and the main content area with the information separated into two columns. I believe that this structure allows recruiters to understand the candidate's profile and find the relevant information they need directly and clearly, without navigating between different pages.

- At the top of the page, the header contains the user's photograph and professional description, which are useful to introduce the candidate. On the other hand, the contact details and LinkedIn link provide ways to contact the user or access his professional profile. I have separated the different topics in order to make a good first impression on recruiters and provide a clear way to evaluate the candidate.

- Below the header, I placed a separate section to show the candidate's name, academic degree, and the levels of the different languages he can use to communicate in professional environments. In this way, the candidate's identity and language skills can be easy to recognize on a first view.

- On the other hand, I have divided the main area into two columns. The left column contains academic background, technical skills, and courses and certifications. In the first section, I thought it would be a good idea to show the overall grades as well as the courses and their durations. Afterwards, I grouped the technical skills into categories, such as programming languages and software tools, to make the information easier to identify. Moreover, at the bottom of the column, we can see the candidate's courses and certifications in more detail.

- Finally, I decided that the right column would contain the user's work experience and additional information that could be useful to recruiters. Each job in the first section includes the position, contract dates, company, and day-to-day work location. Besides, each one contains a list of the responsibilities the user had and the technologies he used. Furthermore, the additional information section presents some interesting topics about the candidate, such as his work availability and soft skills.

- Overall, I feel that the section headings, the combination of different font sizes and typography styles, and the distribution of the website will help visitors understand the information and read the page clearly. As well, I hope that the spacing, alignment, and layout I have used will be useful to keep the CV organized.


> 3. Visual design


- I tried to create a visual design that evokes a professional, clear, and modern CV suitable for the current technology sector. I used a minimal colour palette in order to maintain consistency and help distinguish the different sections clearly.

- I chose dark blue as the main colour of the page, which is used for the header, section headings, and important text. I feel that it provides a good visual structure and contrasts with the white text used on dark backgrounds. Orange is also the colour that helps important elements of the header and the centre section stand out. It highlights the professional profile heading, the user's subtitle, and the language levels. On the other hand, I decided to use light grey for the background of the section cards, while white provides contrast with the dark blue elements and keeps the overall appearance clean. Finally, I used light blue only for the labels in the technical skills section to distinguish them from the main content.

- In order to maintain a visual hierarchy, I chose two typefaces and different font sizes and weights to provide a good structure, distribute the information, and better distinguish between the elements. I used the Barlow font for the main headings, which gives them a distinctive appearance. On the other hand, Raleway is used for the rest of the body text because I believe that it provides a clean and professional style that is easy to read.

- As I mentioned before, the website uses a main header, a centre section, and a two-column layout to show the main content. This arrangement made it possible for me to display all the sections on the same screen while keeping related information grouped together. Furthermore, to achieve this layout, I had to use some CSS properties learnt in class to organize the content of the two columns, place the paragraph beside the professional profile in the header, etc.

- Lastly, in order to help separate content areas and make the website visually attractive, I rounded some card corners, coloured section headings and text, and spaced and aligned the information. As well, I think that displaying technical skills as small labels adds a positive touch to the section because, in this way, individual technologies are easy to identify. I feel the same way about the contact icons and the circular photograph. They give character to the website and make it more comfortable to read.

- From my point of view, I believe that, in general, the design creates a professional impression, is clear and attractive to read, keeps the information consistent and well distributed, and provides quick and easy access to relevant elements.
